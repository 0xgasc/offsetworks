Legal & Compliance Pack (NYC/USA)

Privacy Policy

              
Required the moment you collect a single email. Drafted to cover NY SHIELD Act, CCPA (CA visitors), and GDPR-light (EU visitors). On-brand page, not a copy-paste template.

            

            

              
Terms of Service

              
The site's rules of engagement. Protects you on use of imagery, IP of your characters, governing law, and limitation of liability.

            

            

              
Refund / Return Policy

              
Required to sell in NY and most US states. Sets expectations on damaged-in-transit, pre-orders, edition pieces, and timelines.

            

            

              
Shipping Policy

              
Domestic + international shipping windows, carrier handling, customs/duties disclosure. Required disclosure for ecom under FTC rules.

            

            

              
Cookie Notice

              
Discreet banner + cookie policy page. Covers analytics cookies and any future Shopify/payment cookies. Doesn't ruin the design.

            

            

              
Accessibility Statement

              
WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility statement and a contact route for accessibility issues. ADA / NY State accessibility lawsuits against ecom sites are real and rising—this materially reduces risk.
