Privacy Policy

              
Required the moment you collect a single email. Written to cover the rules in New York, California, and Europe. A real page on brand, not a copy-paste template.

            

            

              
Terms of Service

              
The site rules. Protects you on use of imagery, ownership of your characters, what law applies, and what you are and are not responsible for.

            

            

              
Refund & Returns

              
Required to sell in New York and most US states. Sets expectations on damage in transit, pre-orders, edition pieces, and timelines.

Domestic and international shipping windows, who handles what, and how customs is handled. Required disclosure for selling online.

A small banner and a short policy page. Covers the basics now and any future shop or payment cookies. Does not ruin the design.

            

            

              
Accessibility

              
A clear accessibility page and a way for visitors to flag issues. Lawsuits against online shops over accessibility are real and rising in NY. Having this in place takes a lot of risk off the table.
