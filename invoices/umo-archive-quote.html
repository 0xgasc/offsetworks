UMO Archive

Full-Stack Web3 Concert Archive Platform

A custom-built platform where UMO fans upload, preserve, and celebrate concert moments from across the band's entire touring history. The archive indexes 300+ performances, calculates rarity scores for every uploaded moment, and stores all media permanently on Arweave's decentralized network — time-resistant, censorship-resistant, and free from recurring storage costs. Includes community features, video galleries, content moderation, and a complete admin system. The NFT collectible/monetization layer is built and ready to activate when the time is right. Built from scratch. Designed to last.

Scope of Work

Core Platform

Performance Database
300+ UMO concerts indexed from setlist.fm with city, venue, setlist, and date data. Full search by city, venue, song, or year.
Moment Upload System
Fans upload audio, video, and photos (up to 6GB) linked to specific performances and songs. Resumable uploads for large files.
Permanent Storage
All uploads stored on Arweave via Irys — decentralized, immutable, and permanent. No recurring storage costs. Data persists forever.
7-Tier Rarity Engine
Every upload receives an automatic rarity score (Basic through Legendary) based on file quality, song frequency, and metadata completeness.

Web3 / Decentralized Storage

Arweave Permanent Storage
All uploaded media is stored on Arweave's decentralized network — paid once, stored forever. No monthly fees, no expiration, no single point of failure.
Resumable Large File Uploads
TUS protocol for reliable uploads up to 6GB. Files are verified, hashed, and tagged before being committed to permanent storage.
NFT Collectibles Future Phase
ERC-1155 minting system, revenue sharing via 0xSplits, wallet integration (MetaMask, WalletConnect, Coinbase). Built and tested — ready to activate when the time is right.

What does "permanent storage" mean?

All media uploaded to the UMO Archive is stored on Arweave — a decentralized network where data is paid for once and persists indefinitely. No monthly fees, no single point of failure. If the platform website goes offline, the uploaded concert recordings remain accessible forever via their permanent Arweave URLs.

Traditional cloud storage ~$0.023/GB/month, billed forever
